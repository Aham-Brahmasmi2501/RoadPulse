Ultralytics crack-seg test split (Roboflow Universe, CC BY 4.0): 40 held-out close-up crack photos, polygons converted to boxes, class = crack only. It measures crack detection; it does NOT measure pothole accuracy. The pothole/alligator false positives come from the two dashcam-trained road models firing on concrete texture. For a road-view benchmark run: python scripts/prepare_eval.py --rdd <RDD2022/India/train>
